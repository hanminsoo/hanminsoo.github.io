I run a handful of small services, scheduled jobs and AI coding agents on a single
Mac mini with 8 GB of RAM. Things break in ways the documentation doesn't mention.
This site collects the fixes.

**How these posts are made.** Each post starts from a problem that actually happened
on my machine. The fix is reproduced and checked before publishing — commands are
run, outputs are real. Drafts are written with the help of an AI assistant (Claude)
and reviewed before they go up.

Found a mistake? Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/hanminsoo/hanminsoo.github.io/issues).
